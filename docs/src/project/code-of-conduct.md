# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects contributors and maintainers to keep technical discussion
clear, respectful, and evidence-based. Disagreement about design is normal.
Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, and exclusionary behavior are not.

Examples of expected behavior:

- critique code and decisions, not people
- explain claims with examples, tests, or concrete reasoning
- accept correction when evidence changes
- keep review feedback specific and actionable
- make room for contributors with different experience levels

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- insults, harassment, or deliberate intimidation
- discriminatory or exclusionary language
- repeated bad-faith argument after a decision is documented
- publishing private information without consent
- derailing technical threads into personal conflict

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project-managed communication channels.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the contact route
documented in [Security](security.md). Include links, screenshots, or other
direct evidence when possible so the report can be evaluated quickly.

If your concern is a normal usage question, documentation request, or design
discussion rather than a conduct problem, use the route documented in
[Support](support.md) instead.

If the report is about a vulnerability, leaked secret, or another
security-sensitive defect, follow [Security](security.md) directly and keep
exploit details out of public threads.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or lock content that violates this policy and
may restrict participation for repeat or severe violations. Enforcement should
favor clear explanation and proportional response, but project safety takes
priority over unlimited debate.
